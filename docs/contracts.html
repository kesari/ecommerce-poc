Prove the boundary, then score the answer

Revision 2.2 separates runtime breakage, necessary repair and validation-only work. It does not combine correctness with cost or reward extra searching.

Bottom-up verification

Review criteria and source pins→Run protected business assertions→Reject wrong variants→Freeze reviewed claims and hashes

Baseline, proposed-change and sufficient-repair behavior are executed before models see the cases. Compatible controls need no additional repair; a deliberately incompatible variant checks that their assertions can detect a regression. Compilation or a successful command alone is not runtime proof.

Full records, protected artifacts and exact source snapshots are bound to independently selected hashes. The verifier cannot approve its own changed assertions. Expected answers remain outside the indexed estate.

Five kinds of claim

ClaimWhat it says
Repository runtime statusWhether supported behavior is broken, unchanged or unresolved after the supplied diff and before repair.
Repair obligationWhether the selected strategy needs a production edit, validation only, no impact work or further investigation.
SymbolAn exact repository-qualified baseline member; related class-name prefixes do not count as the same member.
Directed edgeProvider and consumer repository IDs, contract type, original endpoint/topic and relevant field.
ActionA structured change at an attested target: JSON read/alias, topic subscription/publication, or numeric scaling.

A broken behavior may be restored by repairing its upstream input. That does not mean every downstream implementation needs editing. The origin patch is supplied and is not counted again as remediation.

The primary outcome: complete supported impact

A successful answer covers the proved repository observations, causal contracts, repair actions and obligations from one coherent sufficient plan. It needs a complete trusted oracle and valid runner attribution. Missing required claims, malformed findings, established contradictions, unresolved reference claims or unsupported material repair/failure claims prevent complete impact.

Required-action coverage and the earlier bounded-repair outcome are reported separately. Supporting symbols remain diagnostic. Additional claims are unverified unless evidence establishes them as true or contradictory; absence from a reference list does not automatically make them false. An unnecessary edit in a proved no-edit repository is an established error.

The accepted action vocabulary is finite. Unreviewed alternative implementations need independent attestation before receiving equivalent repair credit. Free-text explanations and arbitrary citation truth are not graded by a model judge.

The public scope

Every task covers Java HTTP clients, normal first delivery through registered message listeners, proxy forwarding, web data consumption and private SQL/cache boundaries. Required routes and topics are assumed provisioned; supplied migrations are applied. Deployment, mixed-version rollout, provisioning, security, retries and DLQ handling are outside the evaluated repair. Recommendations for those surfaces belong separately.

H07 explicitly restricts the new whole-INR representation to nonnegative amounts whose original minor-unit values are divisible by 100. H08 explicitly permits prior estimates until the configured cache TTL expires. Its controlled-cache test uses manual expiry and does not measure a real Valkey timer.

Other limits include controlled persistence ports in messaging, controller/loopback boundaries instead of full security deployment, and finite business inputs. Runtime proof is claimed only for the executed boundaries.

Trust and cost stay separate

Semantic oracles establish the reviewed behavior. Protected invocation receipts establish which product outputs reached the agent. The runner stamps case, repetition, arm, model, task, harness, source and index identity before parsing model JSON; an answer cannot choose its own provenance.

Attempts reserve their full cost cap against a durable shared total before a worker starts. Parallel invocations cannot oversubscribe it. A crash retains its reservation; unknown usage remains debited. Insufficient funding leaves the slot unrun, while started failures consume it. Current prepared authorization is zero and paid execution is disabled.

Paired analysis

The design is six families × two cases × four arms × three sessions. Repetitions are averaged inside each case; cases receive equal weight. The analyzer verifies independently sealed execution and budget ledgers and regrades scores against the frozen oracle and schema.

It reports operational success and correctness conditional on valid answers separately. Missing results remain unknown, and failures stay visible. Case-cluster bootstrap intervals, family sensitivity and compatible-control safety constrain any exploratory practical-benefit conclusion; unresolved integrity or exposure blocks it.
