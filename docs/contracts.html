Test data and scores

We decide the correct answer first, hide it from the contestants, and score every result in the same way.

The process

Write the correct blast radius→Freeze it→Run each tool→Compare answers

This prevents us from changing the definition of success after seeing which tool performed best.

What matters most

MeasurePlain-English question
Repository recallDid it find every affected repository? Weighted by severity, so a critical miss hurts more than a label miss.
Contract recallDid it find the relevant REST, Kafka, schema, or API relationship? Also severity-weighted.
PrecisionAcross every finding kind, how much of what it reported was actually affected?
Critical penaltyDid it miss anything that breaks production? Each critical miss lowers the score directly.
EvidenceCan an engineer verify why each finding was included? Every finding carries a tier from compiler-proven to hypothesis, and malformed findings are counted, never silently dropped.
FreshnessCan it see a change that has not been committed or fully indexed?
CostHow much infrastructure and maintenance does it require?

A missed critical dependency is worse than a few extra suggestions. Recall is severity-weighted and every critical miss also lowers a dedicated penalty, so one critical blind spot costs more than several false positives.

How strong is the evidence?

EvidenceExample
Very strongThe compiler proves that this exact symbol is referenced
StrongA REST client matches an endpoint, or a consumer matches a Kafka topic
ReasonedCode, documentation, or configuration implies a business dependency
InvestigateA plausible lead that still needs confirmation

First measured results

Same AI model (GPT-5.4), same read-only tools, same question — the only difference is the reference material. Three runs each, so the pattern below has replicated.

Agent-onlySymbol indexContract graph
Score, address rename (mean of 3)0.810.880.67

Runs with a critical blind spot2 of 30 of 33 of 3

The symbol index found every affected symbol in all three runs and never missed a critical repository; its misses are limited to single repo, contract, or test items. The unaided agent's best run nearly matches the index, but it misses a critical repository in two of three runs — consistency, not ceiling, is what the index buys. The contract-graph runs miss the owning service every time while finding downstream consumers: topology helps downstream, hurts upstream.

On a simpler single-service change, local, hosted, and index-assisted runs all reach about 0.87 by different routes — while the free local model swings from 0.39 to 0.74 on the hard scenario and sometimes answers without searching at all.
