Corpus and scoring

Frozen truth, blind contestant answers, deterministic comparison, and explicit evidence provenance.

Frozen-record-first workflow

Author scenario→Freeze ground truth→Run contestants independently→Validate and score

Ground truth lists affected repositories, symbols, contracts, and required tests before any contestant sees the query. This prevents answers from redefining success after the fact.

Weighted score

ComponentWeightWhy it matters
Cross-repository recall35%The primary completeness measure
Contract recall20%REST, Kafka, OpenAPI, database, and other integration surfaces
Precision15%Limits distracting or untrustworthy findings
Evidence quality10%Rewards classified, inspectable evidence
Freshness10%Measures active and uncommitted change coverage
Latency5%Measures practical response time
Operational cost5%Accounts for indexing and maintenance burden

Weights renormalize when manual freshness or operational-cost inputs are absent. Symbol and test recall remain visible diagnostics. Critical misses are reported separately.
